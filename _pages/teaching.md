With several years of experience in tutoring, I am dedicated to helping students achieve their academic goals. Whether you're gearing up for admission tests to prestigious institutions like Bocconi and Politecnico di Milano, facing high school exams in Chemistry, Mathematics, or Physics, or preparing for the 8th-grade final exam, I'm here to guide you every step of the way.

In my tutoring approach, I prioritize the power of common sense intuition, using practical examples and visual aids such as function plots and physics simulations to make complex concepts more accessible. I understand that each student possesses a unique learning style and individual approach to mathematical problem-solving.

My method involves first grasping the student's existing strategies for tackling mathematical problems. By understanding their current approach, I can then tailor my teaching to align with their preferences and strengths. This personalized approach not only enhances comprehension but also empowers students to apply their own methods effectively.

I offer lessons in both Italian and English, holding a C2 proficiency certificate from Cambridge.

Don't hesitate to reach out for more information. I currently work for [Omnitutoring](https://www.omnitutoring.net/) and tutoring requests should be directed to them.
